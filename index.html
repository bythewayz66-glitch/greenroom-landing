What it is

A rewards platform where the trust layer is not an afterthought

Most GPT platforms bolt anti-fraud on after the first chargeback wave. Greenroom treats validated earnings as the product: every Coin has a ledger row explaining where it came from, every partner postback is validated before it pays, and an account action can never silently confiscate a Coin the user legitimately earned.

🪙

Coins, not points

A fixed peg to cash and an append-only ledger. Balances are always a sum of entries, never a mutable number.

Trust, compliance & anti-fraud

Built against the category's known failure modes

Unjustified bans, voided earnings and payout-verification friction are not edge cases in this category — they are the reason users churn. Each is addressed explicitly, in code rather than in a policy document.

📡

Postback validation

An eight-check pipeline — partner identification, HMAC signature, IP allowlist, duplicate detection, click match, velocity, account standing, amount sanity. Hard failures reject; soft failures park as pending for review rather than destroying a legitimate completion.

📒

Coin-ledger integrity

Append-only ledger with idempotency keys; balances are always derived, never stored. Chargebacks reverse by writing a compensating entry, so a reversal can never corrupt or silently rewrite history.

🔍

Device & IP fingerprinting

A hashed device fingerprint (platform family, screen, timezone, canvas/WebGL hash) builds a device graph. Shared-device clusters are flagged for review — never auto-banned — and the signal is weight-capped below the enforcement band.

🔑

Two-person secret rotation

Re-keying a partner takes a request plus approval by a different admin, rate-limited, with a grace window so in-flight postbacks keep validating and every rotation written to an append-only audit log (fingerprints only).

⚖️

Transparent bans & appeals

User-visible reason codes, a 14-day appeal window decided by a different reviewer, and an earnings-protection rule: validated Coins are never confiscated as a punishment. Hygiene signals never touch a validated balance.

📊

Fraud rate, postback pass rate, false-positive rate and appeal resolution time — the last two being what users actually feel, and what most platforms never publish even to themselves.

Scope & honesty

What this prototype does and does not do

Stated plainly, because a rewards platform that misrepresents its own readiness has already failed the trust test it is built to pass.

Working today

  • Offer wall, catalog, wallet and ledger
  • Four interactive, provably-fair games
  • Live postback receiver with HMAC + IP + dedup
  • Referrals with published vesting
  • Admin fraud console, appeals, device graph
  • Two-person secret rotation with grace window

Not included — and named, not hidden

  • No real money movement. Offer completion is simulated in-app and withdrawals are recorded, not settled.
  • No live partner integrations. Real offerwall postbacks, OAuth and payout rails sit outside the platform's capability set.
  • Admin provisioning is a claim button for demo convenience, not staff provisioning.
  • Email delivery stops at the transport — messages are composed and logged, not sent.

Regulatory note: coin-based games with no purchase sit outside most gambling statutes, but sweepstakes and gambling law varies by jurisdiction. Age gating, regional availability and the automated-decision rules around account actions need qualified legal review before any public launch.
