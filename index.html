🛡 Why you can trust the payout

The rules we wrote down

This category has a bad reputation for good reasons: bans without explanations, earnings that vanish, support that never answers. These are the four commitments built into the platform, not just printed here.

🪙

Balances are derived, never typed

Every coin you hold is the sum of an append-only ledger. Nothing edits a balance — so it cannot silently drift.

✅

Validated earnings are protected

Coins from a partner-confirmed completion are never confiscated as punishment. Only confirmed abuse of the earning itself can be removed.

🛡

A reason code for every decision

If your account is ever reviewed, you get a reason in plain language — not a closed door.

⚖️

A real appeal window

Every decision can be appealed for 30 days, to a reviewer who did not make the original call. Overturning it restores every coin.

What this prototype does and does not do

Stated plainly, because the fastest way to lose trust in this category is a claim a reader cannot check.

🏗

What it does

Runs the full loop for real: accounts, an append-only ledger with derived balances, a signed postback receiver with per-provider verification and an IP allowlist, a reward catalogue, four provably-fair game modes, referrals, an offer and device-fingerprint fraud layer, and a two-person admin rule for secret rotation.

🔌

What needs live credentials

Real partner traffic needs each provider's signing key set in the deployment and its postback configured. The roster is data-driven: a provider flips from “not configured” to live the moment its secret exists — no code change.

✉️

What is not wired here

Email delivery stops at the transport — notifications are composed, addressed, attempted and logged, but the final SMTP hop needs a transport supplied by the deployment. The hourly maintenance scheduler runs in-process; the platform-side cron is not connected in this environment.

📊

About the figures on this page

The coin rate (1,000 = $1.00) and the game RTPs are design values enforced by the platform. The provider and game-mode counts are the real configured totals. The activity ticker illustrates the shape of a validated payout — browse the live prototype for genuine ledger data.

⚠️
Not legal advice.

Game modes are entertainment features with published return-to-player rates, not gambling products. Age gating (18+), regional availability and sweepstakes-law review are prerequisites before live traffic in any market, and automated account decisions are subject to human review on request. Offerwall and survey networks impose their own terms, some of which restrict incentivised traffic or specific geos — get counsel before launch.
